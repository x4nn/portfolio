Flutter Tech & Meet

Last November I attended a Flutter tech meetup at HOWEST, and it was a really fun and interesting experience.

It was great to learn more about Flutter development, hear insights from people working in tech, and meet other students who are also interested in software development. I especially liked seeing how Flutter can be used to build cross-platform apps in a clean and efficient way.

Transition to IPv6

On November 25th last year, I attended a meetup at HOWEST focused on the transition from IPv4 to IPv6.

The session was really interesting because it showed how important networking infrastructure is for the future of the internet. I learned more about why IPv6 is needed, the challenges of switching over, and how companies are handling the transition today.

Since I'm mainly focused on software engineering, it was nice to explore another side of IT and learn something outside of development for a change. Events like these are a good reminder of how broad the tech world really is.

Birth of NATO Cyber Defence

On December 2nd last year, I attended the Tech & Meet session at HOWEST about the birth of NATO's Cyber Defence, presented by Martin De Pauw.

One thing that really stayed with me from the talk was how Cyber Security evolved from being seen as "something for techies" into something that affects everyone today. The session showed how quickly technology keeps evolving and how important cyber resilience has become in a world without digital borders.

I also found it interesting to hear how NATO officially recognized cyberspace as a domain of operations in 2016, alongside land, sea, and air. Learning about the collaboration between military and civilian experts gave a good perspective on how complex modern cybersecurity really is.

As a Software Engineering student, it was inspiring to hear real experiences from someone who helped shape cyber operations within NATO. Definitely one of the more eye-opening Tech & Meet sessions I've attended. 🔐

DeepSeek Uncovered

Recently I attended the "DeepSeek Uncovered" Tech & Meet session at HOWEST, presented by Dimitri Casier.

The session focused on DeepSeek, the open-source AI challenger from China that's becoming a strong competitor to models like GPT-4 and Claude. It was interesting to see live demos of its reasoning, code generation, and multimodal capabilities, especially from a software development perspective.

What I liked most was that the session didn't only focus on the technology itself, but also on the bigger questions around AI: transparency, ethics, and the global competition in AI development. It really showed how fast the AI landscape is changing.

As a Software Engineering student, I enjoy attending talks like this because they help me stay up to date with new technologies and trends that are shaping the future of software development. 👨‍💻
